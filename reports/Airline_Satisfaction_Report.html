Airline Passenger Satisfaction & Churn Mitigation Walkthrough¶

1. Business Objective & Pipeline Architecture¶

Airlines face steep customer acquisition costs, making early detection of dissatisfied passengers critical for targeted retention offers.

This walkthrough inspects the trained production artifact (artifacts/model.joblib), reviews the unified preprocessing and Random Forest + NLP sentiment classification architecture, visualizes feature importance and diagnostic assets, and quantifies expected churn mitigation ROI.
